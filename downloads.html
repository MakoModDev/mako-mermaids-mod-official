🎬 v2.2.6 — The Cinematic Update Latest

  • 🎥 Animated Loading Screen: The loading screen now plays a full video sequence (frame-based, 12 FPS) instead of a static background.
  • 🎬 Ocean Café Cutscene: Right-clicking the Ocean Café Book triggers a one-time intro cutscene before opening the shop.
  • ⏭ Skip Button: Cutscene can be skipped at any time using the "Skip Cutscene" button.
  • 🔁 Replay Button: The Ocean Café shop has a "▶ Replay Intro" button to rewatch the cutscene.
  • 🏪 Auto-Open Shop: After the cutscene ends or is skipped, the Ocean Café trading menu opens automatically.
  • 📊 Loading Progress: Clean percentage indicator on the loading screen (no dark bar, subtle drop shadow).
  • 🐚 Seashells: New items found by brushing suspicious sand.
  • 🐈 Moon Ring: Added new Cat Transformation and Duplication powers.
  • 🧺 Towel: Added 5-second drying mechanic with XP bar countdown.
  • 🐉 Water Dragon: Improved real-time pixel-art boss health bar.
  • 📖 Wiki UI: Overhauled Mako Wiki and Info Hub UI with smooth scrolling and links.
  • 🎨 GUI: Themed Buttons, Patch Notes, More Splash Texts, New Advancements Screen.
  • 🖼 Loadingscreen: Added Mako Island Themed Loading Screen.
  • 🧪 Recipes: The H2O Intro Block now requires: Glas Dolphin, Broken Glas Dolphin, Seahorsepowder, Coconut Smoothie, Arctic Sea Lavender, Moon Crystal, Moon Water Bucket, Moon Crystal Necklace and Arctic Marshmallow.
  • 🌍 Languages: Added german (de_de), message me if you want to help me translate into your language!
